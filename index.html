The Studio

Hello, I'm Mara — founder & lead photographer of MK Studio & Co.

MK Studio & Co was born from a simple belief: that the most beautiful photographs are the ones that feel honest. For over a decade, I've had the privilege of documenting weddings, portraits and personal milestones for clients who want their story told with warmth, intention and a timeless visual language.

Every session is approached like a quiet collaboration — natural light, genuine emotion, and a keen eye for the small details that make a moment unrepeatable.
