About Us

The Story Behind MK Studio & Co

A studio built on light, patience, and an honest love for storytelling.

Meet Mara

Photographer & Founder

I picked up my first camera as a teenager, drawn to the way it could hold onto a moment long after it had passed. That fascination never faded — it grew into MK Studio & Co, a photography studio dedicated to capturing life's most meaningful chapters with honesty and quiet elegance.

Over more than a decade behind the lens, I've photographed weddings on quiet countryside mornings, portraits in golden late-afternoon light, and editorial stories for clients who wanted something that felt genuinely theirs. What ties it all together is the same philosophy: real emotion, unposed moments, and timeless composition over passing trends.

When I'm not shooting, you'll find me scouting new locations, sipping coffee while editing, or planning the next collection for MK Studio & Co.

Mara

Our Philosophy

What Guides Every Session

Three principles shape the way MK Studio & Co approaches every shoot, from intimate portraits to full wedding days.

Natural Light

Every session is shaped around soft, natural light to create images that feel warm, organic and true to life.

Honest Emotion

Minimal posing, maximum feeling. MK Studio & Co favors candid connection over stiff, staged photography.

Timeless Craft

Classic composition and careful editing ensure your images feel just as beautiful decades from now.
